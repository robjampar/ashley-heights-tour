# Six additional options - built concept issue

25 September 2026. Target: seven bedrooms including the loft guest room where used; a separate loft workspace; en suites for most bedrooms; principal bedroom, study, walk-through dressing and bathroom; gym, cinema, wine bar, outdoor pool, double garage and four outside spaces. These are alternatives to, not replacements for, the three saved current designs.

## Internal layouts: keep the current Proposed building envelope

- **I1 Garden kitchen.** Put the main kitchen and family dining together in the rear garden room, with living in the side wing. The old kitchen becomes a working pantry and preparation room. Keep the quiet east lounge, ground-floor office, front gym, front principal suite and basement leisure rooms. Add an en suite to Bedroom 2. Retain the principal bedroom-to-dressing-to-bathroom route and its distinct study.
- **I2 Social east.** Put the main kitchen, dining and living in the east rooms, joining the kitchen/dining wall through a designed structural opening. Move the gym into side garden living and the cinema into the original family room. Replan the old front gym as a ground-floor guest suite; the side bedroom becomes family study space so the total remains seven. Keep the front principal suite. The basement becomes the separate wine bar and games space.
- **I3 Garden principal.** Join the two original east bedrooms with a doorway to form a garden-facing principal bedroom, study and walk-through wardrobe, retaining their principal partition and the existing en suite. Divide the generous proposed front suite into two guest suites. Move the gym into the garden room beside the pool; combine side living and the existing kitchen as the open-plan family room. Keep the east formal lounge, basement cinema and a separate wine bar in the former front gym.

All three need a fourth outside parking bay. This is a site-layout adjustment; their house walls and roofs stay within the current Proposed envelope. Internal changes do not remove the planning risks already present in that envelope.

## Internal and external layouts: retain the original house

- **E1 Retained front.** Keep the original entrance, facade and main staircase. Add a subordinate first floor over the garage and a shallow rear kitchen/living addition. The original family room becomes cinema; part of the east drawing room becomes gym, with a separate wine room beside it. Combine the original east bedrooms for the principal suite; two side bedrooms, a ground-floor guest room and one loft guest room restore the seven-bedroom total. The original east ground-floor room divides into a gym and guest suite with a hall between them. The original 4.95 m-wide garage remains as a tight compact-car double; all six parking positions have sampled entry and exit paths with the others occupied. No basement or roof terrace. The new loft stair has sampled centreline headroom of 2.24 m and near-edge clearance of 1.98 m; reduced-loft provisions need Building Control review.
- **E2 Garden courtyard.** Keep the original front. Put the principal suite in a side wing extending a short distance behind the house, with a low perpendicular garden leisure wing forming a sheltered courtyard. Keep the original four larger bedrooms; add a ground-floor guest suite in the former family room and one loft guest room. The kitchen and former garage become open-plan living; gym and cinema face the garden; the original dining room becomes the wine room. Separate low double garage. No basement or roof terrace. Compare the side/rear mass and neighbour impact carefully with E1.
- **E3 Short forecourt wing.** Retain the additive approach but shorten the front wing and remove its occupied loft and long side dormer. Put principal and guest suites on its first floor; garage, utility and gym below. Four retained original bedrooms and one side bedroom make seven. Use the original loft for workspace only. Put cinema and wine bar below the new wing. Keep a shallow rear kitchen/living addition, with no roof terrace. Test the existing stair connection, lower roof junction and garage approach rather than scaling the current rooms.

## Acceptance before review

Room labels alone are not evidence. Check furniture fit, actual doors and circulation, bathroom access, new kitchen aisles, the route through principal dressing, loft headroom, floors/ceilings, pool and parking geometry. Show retained and altered fabric separately. Distinguish concept checks from surveyed structure, drainage, fire strategy, neighbour daylight and planning decisions. Do not claim that any option is approved or guaranteed permission.
