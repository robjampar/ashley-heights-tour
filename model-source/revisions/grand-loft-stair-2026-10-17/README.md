# Grand loft stair and full-height eaves doors — 17 October 2026

The owner's final direction replaces the earlier request for winders with a broad central stair and separate left/right upper flights. The recent 500 mm outward courtyard extension is reversed; the former upper glazing line at x=10.35 m is retained. The ground-floor glazing remains at x=9.70 m. The larger stair uses the adjoining first-floor landing.

The main flight faces east towards the courtyard windows. It is 1.50 m wide, with nine treads before an intermediate landing at z=4.51875 m. Two 1.25 m branches rise north to the hobby bridge and south to the loft-suite landing. Each branch has five treads plus its final riser. All sixteen rises along either complete route are 171.875 mm. Main goings are approximately 251 mm; branch goings are 250 mm. These are model dimensions, not a construction specification.

The structural opening comprises two rectangles: `[6.95,-3.13,8.66,-1.47]` and `[8.66,-4.32,10.31,-0.28]`. Ceiling meshes and the bridge knee wall are cut with the decks; collision/support surfaces follow the same apertures. The old straight stair is retired. The reading chair and table move clear of the new low soffit. Short handrail returns close both gaps at the intermediate landing; this detail was identified in the actual geometry review.

Both hobby eaves doors use 1.981 m oak leaves with a 10 mm bottom gap. The openings are centred at y=4.50 m, near the ridge, and the leaves swing into the hobby room. Matching linings, planted stops, hinges and architraves are retained. Architraves are scribed to actual roof geometry. Roof lining meshes are excluded from the wall-depth measurement, which previously made these frames too deep.
