# Ashley Heights — 100 improvement proposals

100 local proposals, including 20 larger design alternatives prioritised for owner review; none applied to the approved house.

Approval records your preferred direction. It does not run model changes, commit code or publish. Any material design consequences will be brought back for review.

**Trade-off:** Rails need room to extend while someone stands in front; wet clothes need ventilation.
