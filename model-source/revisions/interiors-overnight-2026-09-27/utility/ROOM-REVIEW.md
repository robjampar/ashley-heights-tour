# Utility review

One 3.2 m fitted east run replaces the opposing fittings. The machines face
the room, with a shared folding surface, two-bin laundry pull-out, deep open
sink and tall cleaning/linen storage. Oak, limestone and ivory are carried
through from the accepted interiors. The west aisle is kept free.

Walls and both windows remain. The garage door is rehung on the other jamb
of its existing opening; the gym door is retained. The sink remains on the
same service wall, adjusted 550 mm south. No WC is added. Final door and
appliance specifications, service allowances and pipework remain to be
confirmed by the project team. The manufacturer references are dimensional
examples, not selected products.

Modelled details include appliance controls, drawer recesses, drum seals,
perforations and lifters; glazed hinged doors; sliding hamper bins; cupboard
shelves, ironing board, broom and iron; a real bowl, strainer and curved mixer.
The working image demonstrates the opening fittings. The default tour shows
them closed; the room doors operate in the full-house tour.
