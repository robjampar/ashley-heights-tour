# Utility — develop one fitted laundry run

Retain the 2.32 × 3.466 m room measured between the modelled wall faces, 900 mm north gym door, 900 mm west garage door
and both existing windows. The east sill is 1.20 m, with glazing from 1.255 to
2.245 m: a standard counter can sit below it without covering the glass.
The south window also stays, with its sill at 1.40 m.
The existing washer/dryer doors face the west wall with very little clearance.
The opposing equipment/cabinet arrangement leaves about 840 mm through the room.

Alternatives: retain opposite runs (reject narrow aisle and wrong appliance
orientation); put appliances on the south wall (possible, but breaks the useful
worktop into shorter pieces); use one east run with both appliances beneath a
continuous folding surface (develop). Keep the wet service on the same east wall,
with a modest southward adjustment of the sink. No WC is added: the owner
previously requested a single utility room.

The 3.20 m run, south to north: washer 600 mm, dryer 600 mm, laundry hamper
500 mm, sink cabinet 800 mm, tall storage 700 mm. Worktop front x 13.025 m
leaves 1.595 m to the western wall face. Both machine fronts face
into that aisle. Their real overall depths and door-open depths guide the
operating check. Allow 60 mm behind the appliance body for connections as a
project assumption, to be checked with the selected installation instructions.

Miele's W1 example is 850 × 596 × 643 mm, with 1077 mm overall depth when its
door is open. These are physical fit references, not a purchasing decision.
[Manufacturer W1 specifications](https://www.miele.co.uk/product/12149050/w1-front-loader-washing-machine-wwd164-wcs-9kg-lotus-white).
A manufacturer T1 manual gives 850 × 596 × 636 mm and 1054 mm open depth, and
allows positioning under a worktop. Model the conservative 643/1077 envelope
until a matched pair is chosen.
[Manufacturer T1 manual](https://www.miele.co.uk/forms2/uk/sa/manuals-125.aspx?asDownload=1&mNo=11365101).

North tall storage stays off the window. Low cabinetry below the window and a
1.2 m folding counter above the appliances support a dirty-to-clean sequence.
A two-bin pull-out hamper, closed detergent storage and separate iron, folded
ironing board and broom storage inside the tall cupboard keep the room quiet. Avoid permanent drying clothes
opposite the appliance doors: they would consume the working aisle.

Use warm oak, limestone, ivory appliance fronts, bronze handles and restrained
under-cabinet light. Model doors, seals, controls, dispenser drawers, sink/drain,
tap and storage clearly. Check the open garage leaf, north door, appliance
doors, hamper and two-person tasks before integration.

The original garage-leaf handing blocked access to the southern working area
when the hamper or sink was in use. Rehang the same 900 mm leaf on the north
jamb of the unchanged opening, still opening into the utility. The sink centre
is 550 mm south of its previous position, on the same service wall. Its basin
is slightly south of the cabinet centre to keep the turn around the open leaf
usable. Both handle sets, not just the leaf, are included in the checks.

Six plan and native-collision states pass with a 600 mm body: ordinary use,
both appliances open, hamper open, sorting with all three open, an occupied
folding position, and an occupied sink. These are separate work states. The
full-open appliance reference leaves 1.183 m to the west wall. The turn around
the garage leaf is about 810 mm before handle effects. This is a concept
circulation check, not an accessibility or construction approval.

The study has 311 authored meshes and three lights. Native detail checks verify
the 715 mm sink bottom, seven samples of each appliance door swing, the 520 mm
hamper extension, and the actual re-hung garage leaf including its handles.
The room model has been visually reviewed closed and in its working state.

The old utility room-label polygon inset its west and south edges by 120 and
76 mm. Use the actual modelled wall faces for this room study and align the
room-label polygon on integration; no wall moves. This is model measurement,
not a measured building survey.

Thin internal plaster returns stop at the inside of both window frames,
keeping Planning’s brick finish outside. The full room-label polygon is
