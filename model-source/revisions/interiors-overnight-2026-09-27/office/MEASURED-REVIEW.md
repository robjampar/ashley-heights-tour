# Ground-floor office

The original bay-window room, hall double doors and structural walls remain. Replace the dark traditional office set with a light oak desk, quiet ivory upholstery, closed storage and a reading chair. The desk is 1.80 × 0.80 m with its long edge north–south: the seated user faces east into the room and receives side daylight from the bay. The screen is approximately 0.80 m from the seated eye, perpendicular to the main bay glazing. Keep the north-east door approach open and storage against the north wall.

Research basis: [OSHA monitor guidance](https://www.osha.gov/etools/computer-workstations/components/monitors) advises placing the screen straight ahead, perpendicular to windows and generally 50–100 cm from the eyes. [Desk guidance](https://www.osha.gov/etools/computer-workstations/components/desks) stresses knee clearance and space for keyboard and pointing device. These guide the furniture concept; the final workstation remains adjustable to the user.

Review journey: enter from hall, see desk and bay, use workstation, approach books/sliding storage, sit in the reading corner. Test chair pullback, storage access and both retained door sweeps. The review page will reuse the established room study controls.

The measured radiator and occupied storage checks favour a 1.80 m desk. The reading side table moves beside the chair’s north arm, leaving a second route past the bay. The plant is set away from both walls so its leaves remain inside the room.
