# Pool terrace and pavilion — measured development

Retain the accepted 8 × 3.5 m pool, sunk spa, +0.20 m terrace and pavilion structure in Proposed. Planning deliberately omits the pool, spa and loggia; it keeps the existing garden buildings, which receive their own interior study. Do not add these omitted structures to Planning as part of furnishing work.

The west deck is only 2.34 m deep. Three 2.05 × 0.78 m loungers face the pool, accessed from their sides with generous gaps between groups. There is no credible walking aisle between their feet and the water. The existing 1.14 m east aisle is the principal pool-to-pavilion route and receives no new furniture. Each lounger has a small side table; the sheltered loggia receives a separate sofa and stone table. Closed towel/cushion storage sits at the north return, clear of the outside-WC approach.

Scale research: Gloster’s Bay lounger is approximately 2.05 × 0.78 m, with several back positions; this supports a realistic full-size concept rather than undersized furniture. This design is an original coordinated model, not a product replica. Source: https://www.gloster.com/en-us/products/collections/bay/bay-lounger/Lopi%20Marble/Teak and manufacturer Grid specification https://www.gloster.com/media/files/en/specsheets/grid_lounger.pdf (the latter is wider, at 0.98 m).

The existing native model has a level defect: the loggia’s intermediate step has been conformed to terrain a second time and ends above the summer-house floor. The level-setting source must be corrected before the final pool route can pass; do not hide this with furniture. Existing sofa parts also straddle the raised summer-house opening; replacing that sofa with a loggia-level group avoids the unsupported edge.

Review uses the established room controls: pool approach, pavilion seating, lounger detail, towel storage, reverse view and measured cutaway. The summer house, changing bench, WC and tool store are the next paired study. Pool cover/safety system, drainage, finishes, services and measured construction remain detailed design work.

Final layout: the three loungers are turned 75 degrees, preserving a continuous paved route on their pool side. All four occupied/use circulation states and 21 native floor contacts pass. The loggia step is corrected to two approximately 172 mm rises with a 370 mm tread.
