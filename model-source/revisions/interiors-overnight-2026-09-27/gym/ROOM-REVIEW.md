# Gym review

The west-facing treadmills, retained glazing and doors establish the cardio
end. The open eastern strip connects the courtyard and utility. Warm grey
rubber, oak storage, ivory walls and restrained bronze lighting continue the
accepted palette without mirrors.

The dumbbell bench has access on both sides; the compact cable station and
eight pairs of weights use the southwest return. Bottles, towels and a rolled
mat have a shallow fitted home. The bike faces west. Rower storage uses two
parts, with the assembled operating area kept separate from running mode.

Detailed controls, safety lanyards, belt landing rails, bike pedal straps,
weight heads, cable handles and rower grille/footplates are real model geometry.
The room is a developed concept: equipment selection, installation, floor
loading, ventilation and electrical design remain for the project team.
The narrow eastern strip is approximately 640 mm before finishes and has
been tested with a 600 mm navigation body; it is not an accessibility claim.
