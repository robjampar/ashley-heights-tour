# Gym — spatial development, not yet integrated

Keep both west-facing treadmills and the explicit no-mirror brief. Preserve the
west gallery door, courtyard doors, utility door and all windows. The northern
treadmills move south slightly to leave an arrival strip behind the courtyard
doors. Their rear safety spaces must be clear of equipment and normal circulation.

Alternatives: retain all old centres (reject: bike/ball in treadmill rear zones,
barbell reaches the east wall); separate treadmills to the two west windows
(reject for now: loses the side-by-side arrangement and breaks up the floor);
two compact west-facing treadmills together with a central flexible strength/
rowing zone (develop).

Reference dimensions use the manufacturer's MyRun manual: 1.76 × 0.79 × 1.26 m
and a 2 × 1 m rear safety zone. The 2.02 m Life Fitness T5 would leave insufficient
room for the eastern passing strip, so it is not interchangeable. A model fit
does not approve a different treadmill. The proposed eastern strip is about
640 mm before minor wall finishes: deliberately record its narrowness.
[MyRun manufacturer manual](https://media.wpd.digital/qicraft/uploads/sites/7/2020/03/MyRun-user-manual-Eng.pdf).

A Concept2 RowErg is 2.44 × 0.61 m assembled, recommends 2.74 × 1.22 m for use,
and separates into a 0.635 × 0.838 × 1.372 m storage group. Show the two-part
storage correctly. Rowing uses the treadmill rear area only while both
treadmills are switched off. The bench stays in place. Running and rowing are
alternate modes; the clear safety zone is never counted as usable while running.
[Manufacturer dimensions and storage](https://www.concept2.com/ergs/rowerg).

The compact oak cable tower references the NOHRD SlimBeam's 2.15 × 0.40 × 0.20 m
body. Its long cable travel does not mean all exercises fit here. The plan's
activity box is our proposed limited exercise area, not a manufacturer's
universal operating clearance.
[Manufacturer dimensions](https://www.nohrd.com/in/slimbeam/).

Carry the palette through oak storage, warm ivory walls, bronze details, simple
linear light and a quieter warm-grey rubber floor. Keep the machinery graphite.
Provide towel and water storage and a proper home for the mat and ball. No
mirror, extra screen or unneeded moving partition.

Plan-stage checks now pass for ordinary use, running, rowing, cycling and
cable work: a 600 mm body can reach the courtyard, utility and water storage
with the gallery and utility leaves open. This checks the drawn boxes only.
Next: model the furniture, verify actual geometry and operating/strength
access, and review eye-level views before integration.
