# Garden guest bedroom — layout development

Preserve the original room, rear window, radiator, balcony opening and ensuite
connection. Continue the accepted warm oak, ivory, limestone and restrained
bronze palette. The existing room has a south-facing bed wall, east wardrobes
and a television standing in front of the rear window.

Compare two credible arrangements: improve the existing south-wall bed with
east storage (limited by the television/window relationship); move the bed
headboard to the uninterrupted east wall, storage to the south wall and a
fixed screen to the west wall (develop). A north-wall bed would cover the
window; a west-wall bed conflicts with the ensuite and balcony openings.

The developing east-wall arrangement uses a standard 1.8 × 2.0 m super-king
mattress in a full 1.92 × 2.24 m envelope, including upholstery/headboard.
South storage is 650 mm deep with sliding fronts. The bed's southern side
has about 855 mm to the wardrobe fronts before detailed trims. The foot
zone leads directly to the ensuite and balcony; a reading chair uses the
rear outlook without covering the radiator.
[Manufacturer mattress dimensions](https://www.hypnosbeds.com/uk/mattresses/super-king-mattresses).

The west wall between the open entrance leaf and the ensuite casing is short.
A fixed 43-inch screen fits there, away from the rear glazing. Check both
reclined eye positions, full screen visibility and neck direction. Its field
of view will be modest at the room's viewing distance; Samsung's 40-degree
immersive target is a screen-size benchmark, not a rule for allowable head
turn. Do not claim these are the same measure.
[Manufacturer viewing guidance](https://www.samsung.com/uk/tvs/tv-buying-guide/what-size-tv-should-i-get/).

Next: draw the two layouts and actual door leaves, check bed sides, wardrobe
use, radiator/window access and the balcony route, then review at eye level.
This is a working study, not integrated or owner-approved.

The refined door arrangement puts the ensuite leaf on its south jamb, opening
out into the bedroom's clear foot zone. This avoids both the WC and the shower
inside the small ensuite. The fixed TV and its console stop below the swing.
The reading table sits farther back, leaving a tested route around the north
side of the bed. The full headboard stays within the 1.92 m bed width, with a
wider oak wall composition behind it.
