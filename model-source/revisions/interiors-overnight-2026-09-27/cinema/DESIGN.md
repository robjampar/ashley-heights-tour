# Cinema: establish the viewing arrangement first

Model shell: 3.94 × 5.143 m, floor −2.80 m, finished ceiling −0.247 m.
Retain the east rear doorway, all partitions and the adjacent AV cupboard.
The previous two rows provided six places but their screen distance and occupied
sightlines need improvement. The rear platform was only 180 mm high.

## Arrangements compared

1. Six fixed seats in two rows: retains capacity, but the room depth forces a
   large difference in viewing distance. Raising the rear row enough to see over
   people adds steps and uses scarce headroom/access space. Do not treat a ray
   between empty seat backs as evidence that occupied rows work.
2. Four reclining chairs in two rows: the manufacturer-dependent extended chair
   envelope further reduces passing and row spacing. Not selected without a
   specific chair that demonstrates fit in both operating states.
3. One generous four-place sofa group with a low table: every seat sees the fixed
   screen directly; the east aisle stays outside the viewing group; a rear arrival
   zone remains clear; no seating platform is needed. This is the developed
   direction. Losing two regular seats is an explicit trade-off, not a hidden
   capacity claim. The owner did not set a minimum cinema seat count.

The drawing uses the full sofa envelope (2.86 × 1.25 m), a low coffee table and a
shallow rear storage cabinet. The screen is 2.60 × 1.4625 m, positioned for the
seating group, 80 mm east of its centre and aligned with the front speakers. Soft warm taupe
upholstery, dark warm acoustic surfaces, restrained oak ribs, low-level lighting
and concealed cables relate it to the accepted rooms without bright surfaces
around the picture.

## Research and application

- THX's home-theatre guidance suggests keeping the view close to screen-centre
  height, within about 15 degrees. We check centre elevation from every modelled
  seat and keep head turn, screen field of view and full-screen occlusion as
  separate tests. [THX home theatre guidance](https://www.thx.com/room-size).
- THX's auditorium guidance uses a 36-degree field of view from the farthest
  seat and unobstructed sightlines. This is a comparison benchmark, not a claim
  of home-cinema certification. [THX screen placement](https://www.thx.com/questions/thx-certified-screen-placement/).
- Dolby begins with a central listening position and points speakers towards
  it, generally at seated ear height for the main layer. Develop a restrained
  5.1.2 concept from that reference; a finished acoustic design still needs
  specialist review. [Dolby 5.1.2 overhead guide](https://www.dolby.com/about/support/guide/speaker-setup-guides/5.1.2-overhead-speaker-setup-guide/).

Checks are recorded in ROOM-REVIEW.md: dimensioned alternatives, the retained
door, occupied seat approach, screen corners/centre, ordinary eye-level views
and source-house preservation.

The initial footrest arrangement was rejected at plan review: it left only a
220 mm slot in front of the sofa. The developed low table leaves a 650 mm
continuous seat approach, with drinks held in the centre console. Normal access
does not depend on moving a footrest.
