# Cinema — room development 01

**Disposition: developed concept for owner review.** Applies to Proposed and
Proposed Planning. The retained 3.94 × 5.143 m shell, 850 mm east doorway and
adjacent AV cupboard remain. The owner has not selected this overnight layout.

The main change is four deep places in one viewing row, replacing six places
in two rows. This deliberately gives up two seats to remove the rear platform
and avoid occupied heads blocking another row. The fixed screen, four-seat
composition, side aisle and quiet rear storage read as one room. The design
uses the house's oak/stone/fabric vocabulary in darker warm tones around the
image. The ordinary entrance, seated, end-seat and reverse views were inspected.

| Review | Evidence and outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Furniture and storage | 2.86 × 1.25 m sofa; four 620 mm places; centre console with drinks and charging; shallow four-drawer rear cabinet |

| Viewing | Screen distance 3.095 m; horizontal field of view 41.49–45.20°; centre elevation 4.35–4.59°; end-seat head turn up to 19.57°. Twenty native rays test centre/corners from four seats |
| Detail | Upholstery seams, lumbar cushions, cup rims, USB-C ports, remote buttons, speaker drivers, projector lens/vents, low guidance light and completed dark doorway finishes are geometry |
| Vertical fit | Ceiling lining occupies 33 mm; projector and speakers sit above the seating; retain the known shell. Seated-eye height is a modelling assumption, not a fit for every person |
| Privacy / sound | Enclosed retained room; dark lining and modelled fabric do not establish sound isolation or acoustic performance |
| Preservation | Full-house mesh/material comparison preserves every retained baseline element, including the accepted kitchen/lounge and principal suite |

Native, circulation and browser evidence lives beside this review and in the
overnight root. Room plans are measured concept drawings. Final acoustics,
ventilation, equipment selection and construction details remain unresolved.
