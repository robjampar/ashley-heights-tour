# Garden guest ensuite — retained compact shell

Keep the L-shaped room and its existing services: shower in the south recess,
WC at the north wall and basin on the west wall. The bedroom connection stays
in the existing opening. Continue limestone, oak, ivory ceramic and restrained
bronze. This is a single-user guest ensuite, not another principal bathroom.

The first comparison is between retaining the present three fixture positions
with lighter fittings, and moving the shower or WC. Moving them adds plumbing
and partition work without improving the compact doorway relationship. Develop
the retained positions, with a shallower floating basin unit and a fully enclosed
shower rather than assuming an 800 mm-deep open shower will contain spray.

A 1200 mm sliding enclosure can have a much smaller opening than its nominal
width: Merlyn's current IQ 1200 specification gives 475 mm. A swing leaf that
opens inward is a better candidate here because it avoids the bedroom door.
[Merlyn dimensional reference](https://www.merlynshowering.com/iq-sliding-shower-door.html).

Matki's EauZone EPW range explicitly supports inward-opening doors. Its EPW1200
uses nominal 700/500 door and fixed-panel modules in a 1170–1200 mm recess.
The published RH CAD drawing shows a 612 mm entry and a 626 mm leaf projection.
These are layout references, not a final product order. The model must allow for
lining thickness, hinge hardware and the actual open leaf, and test the occupant's
position while the leaf closes. The shower controls belong on the back wall so
the inward leaf does not strike them.
[Inward-opening installation manual](https://www.matki.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/INWARD-EPW-INSTRUCTION-BOOKLET-10735-AF-03-25.pdf),
[EPW1200 RH dimensional drawing](https://www.matki.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/EPW-1200-RH.pdf).
