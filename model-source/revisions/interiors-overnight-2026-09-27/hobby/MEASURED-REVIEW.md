# Original-house hobby loft, eaves and bridge

Both isolated studies now include a two-person 2000 × 1050 mm project table,
lockable castors, real tool details, sliding supplies storage, low archive cabinets,
books, an ivory sofa, a curved reading chair and limestone tables. The open centre
can take a temporary project. The main floor, retained bridge and landing receive
a consistent oak finish, with shallow relief art and low lighting in the passage.

The roof, windows, stairs, guards and all openings remain. Eaves doors are new
plain oak leaves in the retained low apertures, opening into the stores. Low shelves
fit below the roofs. They remain stooping storage, not standing rooms.

Native checks pass in both variants: complete furniture footprints, sixteen chair-leg
and castor contacts with the actual floor finish, both door assemblies through 91
positions each, and furniture against the actual roof. Seven separate use states
pass with a 600 mm body. A separate 600 mm-body line beside the stair guard remains
navigable, but does not have 2 m headroom.

## Bridge constraint requiring review

clearance along that line (sampled with a 200 mm head footprint). The minimum
measured value is 1.783 m. This is an existing geometry constraint, not resolved by
furnishing the space. It needs architectural/structural resolution before construction.
The study intentionally preserves the roof and stair envelope for owner review.
No claim is made that this is a full-height route for taller people.
