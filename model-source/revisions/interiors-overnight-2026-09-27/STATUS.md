# Overnight interiors — publication checkpoint

Cinema and wine bar/games are developed for review in both Proposed and Proposed
Planning. The accepted kitchen/lounge and principal suite are unchanged. The
full native models, walkthrough exports and isolated room studies agree.

- Cinema: 156 new mesh parts, two native lights; four seats in one row.
- Bar/games: 505 new mesh parts, five native lights; three bar stools, clear
  serving aisle, fixed TV group and separated pool/darts activity zones.
- Full native checks: every authored mesh matches the isolated study; 20 cinema
  and 15 bar TV rays pass in each design.
- Full navigation: cinema seat/storage/aisle routes and bar ordinary/occupied/
  open-cooler routes pass. Retained principal-suite routes also pass.
- Browser: both designs, all isolated cameras, cutaway, mobile, full-house room
  views, detailed parts and retained doors pass without page/asset errors.
- Preservation: 13,145 retained Proposed meshes and 12,330 Planning meshes are
  unchanged, with zero vertex differences and identical materials.

Trade-offs: four cinema seats replace six; the 650 mm seat approach requires
people to tuck their knees in when another person passes. The pool uses real
6 ft British dimensions; reserving its full cue area leaves about 622 mm single-
file passage beside the stair. These are developed concepts, not owner approval
or construction/acoustic/ventilation certification. See the individual reviews.

The gym is a separate unintegrated working study. Its occupied-use tests are
still being refined; it is not included in this published room update. Other
remaining rooms are unchanged at this checkpoint.
