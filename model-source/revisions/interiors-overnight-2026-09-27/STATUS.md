Trade-offs are explicit in the room reviews. Four cinema places replace six;
passing along the 650 mm seat approach requires occupied viewers to tuck in.
The pool's full cue area leaves about 622 mm single-file passage by the stairs.
The gym's rear treadmill run-off is two metres; rowing uses that area only with
both treadmills off. Its eastern passage is about 640 mm and single-file. The
previous barbell rack and loose ball were removed. Utility working states are
checked separately; appliance/service selection and the garage-door specification
still require detailed design. These are developed concepts, not owner approval
or construction, acoustic, ventilation or accessibility certification.

The guest wardrobe is shorter than the previous 4.24 m run. Its fixed 43-inch TV
has a modest view at this distance. The compact ensuite is for one person at a
time; the 612 mm shower entry and inward leaf require final product, stop and
site verification. The 85-degree stop protects the handle from the tiled return.

The family-lounge desk is reduced from 1.60 m to 1.14 m frontage, with a deeper
600 mm surface and side daylight. Its storage-front passage is single-user.

The cloakroom's shortened vanity and wide drawer service cutout reduce storage.
Its approximately 679 mm retained doorway and shared fixture-use space are for
one person at a time. The model does not establish an accessible layout.

Bedroom 2 gains wardrobe frontage and depth. Its reading chair is for upright
use, without an extended footrest. Occupied bedside and reading routes remain
single-file. The small existing loft-base projection is wrapped in ivory on its
exposed bedroom faces; the underlying loft geometry is preserved.

Bedroom 3 retains a single-file foot route of about 679 mm to the radiator.
The wardrobe approach is also single-file. The initial perch position blocked
arrival when occupied; its final position is 500 mm farther north.

The family bathroom uses separate fixture-use states and remains a compact,
single-user room. Its shorter vanity prioritises bathing and circulation.

Bedroom 4 and its ensuite have a paired working plan with seven circulation
states. The draft retains a full-size double and proposes moving the shower
return 113 mm west for an 800 × 900 mm enclosure. Native geometry and door
clearances are not yet verified; this draft is not in the published full model.
