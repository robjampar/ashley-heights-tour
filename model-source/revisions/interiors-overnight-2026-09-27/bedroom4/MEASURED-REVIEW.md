# Bedroom 4 and ensuite — paired development

The main bedroom is about 4.10 × 3.01 m internally, with a north-east entrance neck. The existing mattress is a full-size double, approximately 1.37 × 1.91 m. The working alternative initially tried a king; that blocked the bed-foot route with the ensuite door open. The developed candidate keeps a 1.35 × 1.90 m double in a complete 1.49 × 2.06 m frame, backed against the solid north wall.

A 2 m long, 650 mm deep west wardrobe replaces the old approximately 885 mm wide, 340 mm deep unit. Sliding fronts, shelves and hanging rails are modelled. The shallow bedsides protect the entrance turn. The full frame leaves about 819 mm to the front radiator and a 640 mm single-file west storage/bedside approach. The front window and radiator remain. A recessed Roman blind replaces the heavy curtains. The old upper cupboard and ornaments are removed with the old wardrobe.

The existing ensuite shower was approximately 680 × 710 mm. The candidate uses an 800 × 900 mm tray. Its west return moves 113 mm west, with a rebuilt infill at the linen-cupboard back wall and shortened jog. Both existing door apertures stay in place; the hall leaf opens inward and the ensuite leaf opens into the bedroom. This is a local partition alteration, not a finish-only change.

The WC stays near the north-west services and the new 900 × 400 mm vanity stays on the south wall. The former front window is already blocked in both proposals by `Proposal | Bricked-up landing side light`; the new limestone and mirror finish its inside face. No new opening is assumed. The WC allowance is 380 × 490 mm, based on a real compact 370 × 480 mm ceramic; the 190 mm cistern enclosure allows for a 155 mm installation frame plus lining and allowance. Product basis: [Duravit D-Neo Compact 258809](https://pro.duravit.co.uk/pro/content/homepage/products/categories/overview/products~402880943a1b6e1b013a1bd2494e0047.uk-en.html?ncat=toilets&nser=0998&product=6661051).

The paired plan checks seven separate states using a 600 mm body. Detailed native door sweeps, drawer travel, wall continuity and final full-house integration are being checked; consult the completed audit files before treating this draft as published. The bathroom is a single-user room, with final enclosure/stop, services, waterproofing, ventilation and measured site dimensions to be resolved.
