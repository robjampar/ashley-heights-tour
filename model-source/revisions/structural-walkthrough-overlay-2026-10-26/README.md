# Direct structure overlay and split comparison — 26 October 2026

The owner requested the structural layer directly over the normal walkthrough, with opacity or split comparison, and then a stronger colour option. This supersedes M12/M13's grey-material architecture presentation. The model, camera and walk/fly navigation are shared; activation does not move the user or load a second house GLB.

The compact **Structure on the house** card provides Overlay / Split view, independent 0–100% house and structure opacity, **Vivid structure**, Layers & cutaways, and the existing drawings/calculations/costs. Split is a draggable screen-space wipe of the same full-size camera projection, not two independently moving cameras. Arrow keys adjust the divider. Its left side shows the finished house; its right side shows the overlay. House cutaway height applies to the house image on both sides; its opacity control affects the overlay side only. Structure opacity zero and the finished side of the split do not pick invisible members. Controls can collapse, including on mobile.

The normal lighting composer renders the house with its original materials, texture maps, daylight, room exposure and mirrors. A separately lit member pass is composited over that image. Opacity is applied to the entire member image, avoiding accumulation from overlapping transparent brick/joist faces. Vivid changes saturation and contrast only in the structural image. Cutaways temporarily append the architecture clipping plane to the actual materials; returning to full height or switching Structure off restores the original clipping arrays and material identities. Member picking updates camera/object transforms before raycasting, including immediately after Focus in 3D. A member deep link opens its inspector. The standalone orbit studio remains available.

Sources: `walkthrough/src/structure-walkthrough.js`, `structure-composite.js`, shared `structure-studio.js`, and the existing main/lighting entries. Build with `make build-web`. Original source recovery is in `original/`. No native architectural model or publication was changed by this presentation work.

Browser evidence and reproducible review scripts are in this directory and `walkthrough/test-results/structural-walkthrough-overlay-2026-10-26/`. The review exercises camera preservation, material identity, opacity, divider mouse/keyboard control, picking, walking/flying, cutaways, drawings, foundation facts, cost data, mobile panels, returning to the finished model, member deep links and the standalone studio. Screenshots of overlay, split, member inspection, vivid beams and mobile controls were visually inspected. Raw browser request diagnostics are retained: Chrome intermittently reports `net::ERR_ABORTED` for completed GLB loads, also observed before this change. This is not described as a zero-network-error result; the browser report distinguishes these from unexpected failures.

The associated geometry follow-up is [M14 west loft and front walls](../structural-west-loft-front-2026-10-26/README.md). Structural colours and successful UI checks do not verify structural capacities.
