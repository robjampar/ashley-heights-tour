# Bathroom and walk-through wardrobe — design before implementation

The owner accepted the south-wall bedroom and requested the bathroom and walk-through wardrobe next. Keep that bedroom and the existing external openings. Fit the 24.5 m² northern wing; review it as an isolated editable suite for both proposals.

## Research and room decisions

[Duravit's floor-plan guidance](https://www.duravit.com/en-us/planning-inspiration/magazine/ideas-design/bathroom-floor-plans/) prioritises services, full fixture dimensions, access, lighting and screened toilet positions. These are design checks, not UK statutory clearances. [Luv 1800 × 800](https://www.duravit.com.au/bathtubs/700434000000000) supplies a credible full-size freestanding-bath envelope; the model is a bespoke concept using that size, not a licensed product CAD or final specification.

Put the new divider just north of the east window, at y=-6.95. A divider across that window is unacceptable. Wardrobe remains dry and separately closable from the wet room. Roughly equal areas give substantial dressing storage without an excessive wardrobe or token bathroom.

Check a 1 m dressing route after the 950 mm opening, including the rounded turn between wardrobe corners. A 950 mm bathroom opening gives about 928 mm at the open leaf before final ironmongery. Test a 900 mm bathroom route, actual open drawers and two users. Shower opening is 900 mm nominal, reduced slightly by glass edge. The WC return screens the approach; it is not a separate acoustically enclosed room.

Model hollow sanitaryware, taps and controls, drains, joints, mirror surrounds, towel storage, working-depth wardrobe carcasses, rails, hangers, clothes, drawers, shoes and lighting. Keep the window clear. No island or loose stool in the route.
