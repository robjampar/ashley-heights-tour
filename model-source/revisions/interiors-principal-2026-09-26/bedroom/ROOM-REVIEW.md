# Principal bedroom — room development 02

Disposition: **ready for owner review as an isolated bedroom**, with adjoining dressing/ensuite development still required. This is the current furnished bedroom direction for Proposed and Proposed Planning. It has not replaced either whole-house suite.

Owner requirements: a bed against a real wall, straight-ahead TV in bed, a separate sofa TV area, a useful but smaller desk, generous dressing/ensuite space, a modern oak/limestone/ivory interior. No pivoting TV. Internal partitions may change. This development uses the existing south wall and two fixed screens. It does not present an unselected earlier sketch as an owner-approved plan.

Evidence: saved compact and planning native models, their navigation/shell geometry, generated room models and seven browser views per design. Dimensions remain model-based; a site survey, structural/service verification and final products are outstanding.

Design judgment: the bed has a calm, complete wall composition and the sofa has its own understandable purpose. The desk is useful without creating a separate large office. The principal compromise is the long bed viewing distance and relocation of the existing bathroom. These should stay visible in the next iteration rather than be hidden behind a generic “pass” score.
