# Circulation-first study — set aside

Owner feedback on 18 October 2026 clarified two failures in the preceding options: no clear route to the principal bedroom, and awkward positions for the first step. The subsequent quarter-turn study protected a separate east-side bedroom passage and aligned the first flight with the existing landing. The owner then said: “the geometry is too complicated to build”. This scheme is set aside, not accepted for integration.

The new study used 1.10 m wide flights, an approximately 1.10 m square turn landing, eleven approximately 198 mm rises, 312.5 mm lower goings and 250 mm upper goings. It retained the 570 mm lower loft gallery with three steps up into each room. The internal flat ceiling was at 7.08 m, giving 2.10 m gallery headroom. The nominal 180 mm deck left 2.00 m below. It assumed local removal/reconstruction of wall heads, ceiling layers and wardrobe tops. These unresolved structural junctions and multiple levels remain substantial compromises.

The final isolated mesh audit sampled a 900 mm swept envelope along the first-floor principal-bedroom route and stair approach, floor continuity and overhead clearance, plus stair/upper route headroom. It passed the sampled checks (393 bedroom-route samples, 75 approach samples, 450 stair/upper/below-gallery headroom samples). The retained loft-suite doorway remains approximately 1.97 m clear and is separately recorded. These tests are not structural verification, construction approval or owner acceptance. Full upper-route body-collision and final screenshot review were not completed before the complexity feedback.

Live browser review covered the first-floor plan, a lower-floor cutaway, the first-step eye-level view and the bedroom passage at 50% route progress. The principal doorway was visibly reachable along the passage. Existing-main-stair handrail clearance prompted a small 150 mm route adjustment, without moving that staircase. Simplified materials and cutaway clipping make this a spatial study, not a finished architectural rendering.

The builder reads the existing compact export but writes only study outputs. Its source SHA256 check confirmed the source model unchanged. Planning/native/main walkthrough files were not changed.
