# Five loft stair concepts — 18 October 2026

The owner requested five drawings following the flush-courtyard discussion. `five-loft-stair-options.html` is the inline comparison: all five plans are drawn at the same scale, with a 3D stair-shape view and an optional existing-low-roof overlay. `layouts.json` contains the concept geometry.

1. Broad U-shaped return: 1.20 m flights and a shared upper landing.
2. Reversed grand split: 1.50 m central flight, two 1.25 m upper flights and longer landings at both ends.
3. L-shaped quarter-turn: two 1.20 m flights against the inner side.
4. Straight flight and gallery: 1.30 m width and one continuous ascent.
5. Three-flight open well: 1.10 m flights, two intermediate landings and a small central void.

All concepts hold the courtyard facade at x=9.70 m. Their nominal passage targets are approximately 1.00–1.15 m; final wall finishes, rails and junctions still need coordinating. Sixteen 171.875 mm rises span the model's 2.75 m floor difference. Flight goings are 250–300 mm in these diagrams. Each option requires roof/headroom and floor-opening work. Option 2 also needs longer upper landing connections. None is represented as a completed structural or construction design.
