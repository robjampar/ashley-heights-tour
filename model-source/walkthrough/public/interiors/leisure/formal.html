Two generous three-seat sofas face across a slim limestone table, with the existing fireplace at one end. Eight places around one table.

The owner-approved 1.70 m round oak pedestal table is centred in the hexagonal dining area, with eight upholstered chairs and a re-centred pendant. The existing lounge and drinks cabinet are retained.

Detail the room around movement.

New oak hall leaves use room-side hinges in the existing openings, with different opening stops to suit each return. The chair footprints and 400 mm pullback fit the dining bay. Passing and serving beside the west drinks cabinet remain tight when chairs are occupied; furniture fit is not a claim of unrestricted circulation.
