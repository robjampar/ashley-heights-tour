GUEST ENSUITE / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

A compact bathroom, carefully fitted.

A floating oak basin unit, limestone shower and restrained bronze fittings.

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

Keep the useful arrangement.

The shower stays in the south recess, the WC below the north window and the basin along the west service wall. A 900 mm floating oak unit, recessed ceramic basin and lit mirror create a cleaner room. The existing window remains; a simple linen blind replaces the heavy curtains.

Make the door movements work.

The bedroom door opens outward. The shower door opens inward to an 85-degree stop, with its double-sided handle clear of the wall and room to stand inside while closing it. The shower controls are on the back wall, away from the moving glass.

A small, single-user room.

The shower entry is approximately 612 mm wide and remains tight. The basin and WC share standing space. The layout checks these uses separately and tests a 600 mm body through the shower. This is a developed concept: the actual enclosure, opening stop, waterproofing and services need selection against measured site dimensions.
