Let the roof shape the room.

Keep the standing routes in the taller space.

The 1.50 × 2.00 m king bed sits against the bathroom partition. Both sides and the foot have checked routes beneath the measured roof. A compact 205 mm bedside perch beside the ensuite preserves its doorway; the opposite bedside is larger. The solid bedroom entrance wall stays in place.

Use the lower space for storage.

A 1.97 m sliding wardrobe occupies the entrance wall, with hanging rails, shelves and folded-linen storage. The low eaves cupboards and hip-store shelving have been removed at owner review, leaving these floor areas clear. The hip store retains its low doorway and sloping roof. Oak, ivory, wool and a restrained green plant continue the house palette.

Fit the bathroom to the dormer.

The roof, windows, partitions and door apertures remain. The basin and WC keep broadly familiar service positions. A low shower tray and lower rainfall fitting suit the approximately 2.09 m ceiling. The shower leaf opens inward and the hip-store door opens into the bathroom to avoid the roof. Complete door sweeps, roof fit and separate circulation states are checked in the native models. Final waterproofing, fittings, service routes and site dimensions need detailed design.
