BEDROOM 4 & ENSUITE / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

Make a smaller room work well.

A wall-backed double, useful fitted storage and a carefully rearranged compact ensuite.

Loading your room…

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

Keep a proper bed and a clear arrival.

The 1.35 × 1.90 m double sits against the solid north wall in a complete upholstered frame. Shallow floating bedsides, individual reading lights and charging controls sit on an oak backdrop. A 2 m sliding wardrobe provides proper 650 mm-deep hanging and shelf space. The front window and radiator remain.

Give the ensuite a more useful shower.

An 800 × 900 mm shower replaces the previous approximately 680 × 710 mm tray. This requires moving the short return 113 mm west and rebuilding its connection to the linen-cupboard back wall. Both door apertures remain; the ensuite leaf opens into the bedroom. A 900 mm floating vanity and compact concealed-cistern WC keep the main service positions.

Be honest about a compact pair of rooms.

A king bed blocked the route with the bathroom door open, so the new bed retains the original double size. The full frame leaves about 819 mm to the radiator, with a 640 mm single-file west aisle. Seven use states are checked with a 600 mm body, alongside complete door sweeps, drawer travel and the wall junction. The ensuite is for one person at a time. Selected fittings, enclosure stop, services and measured site dimensions still require detailed design.
