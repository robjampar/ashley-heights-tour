LOFT HOBBY ROOM & BRIDGE / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

Space to make, read and change things.

A movable oak worktable, useful low storage and a relaxed sitting group leave the centre open for different hobbies.

Loading your room…

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

Give making a proper place.

The 2.00 × 1.05 m project table has two regular chairs, lockable castors and a clear approach. Supplies sit in sliding cupboards below the dormer windows. Low archive storage follows the opposite roof slope, with books gathered near the sitting area.

Keep the middle flexible.

An ivory sofa and curved reading chair face a small limestone table at the east end. The middle stays clear for a changing project. Oak floors, linen blinds, small plants and restrained bronze details continue through the room, bridge and landing. The windows, roof, stairs and guards remain.

Recognise the low areas.

The eaves doors retain their low openings and lead to stooping storage. The retained bridge beside the loft stair is also constrained: along the usable route, measured clearance is approximately 1.78–1.90 m. The room furnishings and doors fit, but this bridge is not a full-height route for taller people. Its headroom needs resolving in detailed design before construction; this study retains the existing roof and stair geometry for review.
