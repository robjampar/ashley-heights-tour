SIDE-WING BEDROOM & ENSUITE / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

Make a compact suite feel settled.

A wall-backed double, useful fitted storage and a limestone ensuite, with a clearer route between them.

Loading your room…

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

Put the bed on a solid wall.

The 1.35 × 1.90 m double turns against the solid east wall, with a slim upholstered frame, oak backdrop and two floating bedsides. There is 940 mm beside the south bed edge and 835 mm beside the north edge, plus a 665 mm route at the foot. A shallow luggage bench sits beneath the west window.

Release the corner by the door.

The bedroom entrance partition moves north into the family approach; its main seating arrangement stays intact. The ensuite east partition moves 350 mm west of the original layout. The bedroom door keeps its 850 mm opening. A 1.11 m sliding wardrobe provides 590 mm-deep storage and ends clear of the west window.

Keep the bathroom practical.

The one-metre shower and WC keep their service positions. A shallower 360 mm basin unit moves with the partition and has sliding oak fronts, an open bowl, lit mirror and bronze fittings. All three complete doors and the rebuilt junctions are checked, along with seven separate use states using a 600 mm body. This remains a compact single-user ensuite, with final services, enclosure hardware and site dimensions to be detailed.

Developed concept for review. Dimensions come from the model. Furniture access, viewing and simultaneous-use checks are recorded separately from construction feasibility.
