GYM / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

Space to train, room to move.

Two gate-facing treadmills, a clear entrance and useful oak storage.

Loading your room…

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

A calmer training room.

Warm grey rubber, natural oak and ivory carry the house palette into the gym. A full-size dumbbell bench, eight pairs of weights and a west-facing bike have their own places. The extra cable tower is omitted to keep the utility route open. Towels, water and a rolled mat are gathered beside the utility. The windows remain clear and the room has no mirror.

Two exercise modes.

Both treadmills have a clear two-metre run-off behind them. The rower is stored in two parts in the default model. For rowing, both treadmills are switched off and the marked rear floor area is used instead. The bench stays in place. The measured plan shows these distinct arrangements.

The practical limits.

The hall door moves 300 mm south, with the neighbouring internal window 200 mm south. The courtyard route passes east of the rear safety zones. Running, rowing, cycling and bench exercises have been checked as separate activities; this is not a claim that every station can operate together. The previous barbell rack and loose ball have been removed to improve access. Final equipment, selected exercises, fixings, floor loads and ventilation require detailed review.

Developed concept for review. Dimensions come from the model. Furniture access, viewing and simultaneous-use checks are recorded separately from construction feasibility.
