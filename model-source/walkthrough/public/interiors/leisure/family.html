A settled sitting area.

The three-seat ivory sofa sits fully against the solid south wall, with individual reading lights and a simple relief artwork. Low limestone tables, warm oak and a soft wool rug continue the house palette. Books, games and a small plant sit in shallow fitted storage.

A useful place to write.

The north-facing desk receives light from the west window at the side. Its rounded oak top, upholstered chair, task lamp and charging points suit occasional writing or homework. At 1.14 m wide it is smaller than the previous 1.6 m desk, with a deeper 600 mm surface.

Keep the routes clear.

The table shape and positions leave access around the sitting group, including with the chair pulled back or the sofa occupied. Proposed retains its terrace doors with fitted handles; Planning retains its north window and has no terrace. The narrow space at the storage front is for one person at a time. The existing room shell, landing connection and bedroom doorway stay in place.
