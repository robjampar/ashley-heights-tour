Useful storage, room to move.

Warm oak, concealed linen storage and a quiet reading corner connect the bedrooms without filling their routes.

Let circulation lead.

The reading chair and stone table sit beneath the loft stair, with a cordless bronze table lamp and a clear approach in front. A shallow bookcase uses the upper gallery wall beside the principal-suite approach. The path along the entrance void and the approach to the loft stair stay open, including with the chair occupied.

Put storage in the wider space.

The connecting landing receives 2.10 m of linen storage with sliding fronts and a coordinated wall of books, objects and closed cupboards. The retained bedroom openings stay clear. Individual books, folded linen, cupboard tracks, pulls and chair upholstery details are modelled.

Keep the retained architecture.

Oak flooring and quiet relief art connect the first-floor route. The stair openings, voids, radiator, glazing and structural walls remain; the original stair receives coordinated oak and bronze finishes. The isolated study checks furniture fit, floor support, door movements, sliding panels and occupied routes. The separate low-headroom loft bridge remains flagged in its own study.

Developed concept for review. Dimensions come from the model. Furniture access, viewing and simultaneous-use checks are recorded separately from construction feasibility.
