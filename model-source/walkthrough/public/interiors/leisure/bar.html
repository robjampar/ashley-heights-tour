WINE BAR & GAMES / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

Room to serve, sit and play.

A fitted oak and limestone bar, a comfortable TV group and separate spaces for pool and darts.

A usable serving space.

The two-metre bar keeps three places, with upholstered stools, a bronze footrail and a proper knee overhang. Behind it, a 1.3 m clear working aisle gives access to the wine cooler, drinks cooler, drawers and glass storage. This is a dry bar; glass washing stays in the utility or kitchen.

Each activity has its own place.

A standard 6-foot British pool table turns across the room, with full-length cue space around the playing surface. The darts zone sits separately between the pool and bar, away from the cinema route. The west-facing sofa has its own fixed TV and controller table.

The space that remains tight.

With the full pool-playing area reserved, the passing strip beside the stairs is about 620 mm wide. It works as single-file access, not a broad principal route. The measured plan shows this alongside occupied stools and the separate darts area. Equipment selection, delivery, acoustics and ventilation still need detailed review.
