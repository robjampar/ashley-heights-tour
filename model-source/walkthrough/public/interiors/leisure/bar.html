WINE BAR & LOUNGE / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

Parisian Art Deco, at home.

Cream fluting, warm marble, walnut and burgundy velvet bring the selected Parisian Art Deco concept into the basement.

Loading your room…

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

The island is the heart of the room.

The original bar-height island and three high stools are dressed in cream fluting, veined Calacatta Oro marble, burgundy velvet and satin brass. Antique mirrored wine displays, lit walnut shelves, glassware and drinks coolers complete the back wall.

Furnish the whole room.

Two burgundy club chairs and marble side tables join the sofa and TV group. Walnut cabinetry, moulded cream walls, geometric prints, ribbed glass lights, books and greenery carry the same detail throughout the room. A second marble-topped high table adds two matching stools where the dartboard stood. Darts now sits beside pool; the two games share playing space and are used alternately.

Keep everyday access.

The original 1.30 m aisle remains between the island and drinks cabinet. The west-side console is shallow enough to preserve the route around the island. With the pool-playing area reserved, the passing strip beside the stairs remains about 620 mm. The plan shows the retained island and stools alongside the extra lounge furniture.

Developed concept for review. Dimensions come from the model. Furniture access, viewing and simultaneous-use checks are recorded separately from construction feasibility.
