ENTRANCE & GROUND-FLOOR HALLS / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

A calm arrival, a clear route onward.

Useful coat and shoe storage, a softly lit double-height entrance and simpler fittings in the original hall.

Loading your room…

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

Give everyday things a place.

Two 320 mm-deep oak and ivory joinery legs wrap the wall corner. End-on hanging keeps coats within the shallow cabinet; the return has a bag bay, drawer and stone key shelf with charging. A separate 1.43 m shoe bench sits below the retained gym window. Occupied coat and bench positions leave the onward route clear.

Keep the architecture open.

The front doors, entrance link, glass-lined garden passage, basement stair opening and upper guards stay in place. Three restrained opal pendants hang from the measured sloping roof, above the walking route. Limestone flooring and a single plant connect the entrance with the original hall. The cutaway removes the upper storeys to reveal this ground-floor sequence.

Simplify the old hall.

The original steps keep their geometry, with oak treads, ivory risers and simple bronze balusters beneath oak handrails. The radiator and door openings remain. Plain stone niche shelves, small ceramics and bronze/opal lights replace the ornate fittings without recutting the wall apertures. A narrow full-height mirror and shallow relief artwork complete the hall. Complete door sweeps, drawer travel, furniture fit and occupied routes are checked; final joinery, electrics and site dimensions need detailed design.
