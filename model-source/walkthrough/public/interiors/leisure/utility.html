A calm, practical laundry.

Everything in a useful sequence.

The washer and dryer face into the room beneath a shared folding surface. A pull-out laundry hamper, undermount sink and tall storage complete the run. Oak, limestone, ivory appliance fronts and bronze details continue the house palette. The existing window brings light above the worktop.

Small changes, better movement.

The room retains its walls, window and both door openings. The garage door is rehung on the other jamb so its open leaf stays away from the working area. The sink remains on the east service wall with a local position adjustment. There is no WC.

Checked in use.

The aisle measures 1.595 m to the worktop. Open appliance doors, the pulled-out hamper, folding and sink use have each been checked with a 600 mm navigation body and the room doors open.
