ROOF TERRACE · PROPOSED ONLY / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

An outdoor room with space around it.

Pale timber, ivory cushions and honed stone form a relaxed sitting group and a separate four-seat café table. This terrace exists in Proposed only; Planning retains its roof and family-lounge window.

Loading your room…

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

Make two comfortable places.

A two-seat sofa faces two individual lounge chairs, with a small oval stone table between them. The round café table provides four regular places. Slatted timber frames, shaped back rails, cushion seams, fixings, lamps and tableware are modelled in the same restrained palette as the house.

Keep the rooflights and routes clear.

The existing terrace doors, three flush rooflights, stone surface, guards, west privacy screen and drainage remain. Furniture is set back from the outer guards and stays off the rooflights. Checked routes reach every seating group and the perimeter without crossing glass. Low cushion storage and two modest planters sit near the building.

Preserve the differences between the proposals.

This study is available for Proposed only. It does not add a terrace to Planning. The furniture layout is checked in ordinary and occupied use; roof construction, loading, wind restraint, waterproofing, guard and rooflight details still need project-specific design. Cushions should be stored indoors in bad weather.

Developed concept for review. Dimensions come from the model. Furniture access, viewing and simultaneous-use checks are recorded separately from construction feasibility.
