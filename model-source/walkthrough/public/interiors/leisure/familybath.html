FAMILY BATHROOM / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

A calmer room for everyday bathing.

A full fitted bath, floating oak vanity and restrained limestone and bronze details.

Keep the useful service layout.

The bath stays beneath the north privacy window, with the basin on the west wall and WC on the east. A 1.20 m floating oak vanity replaces the previous 1.73 m cabinet run. Its recessed basin, service-cutout drawer, lit mirror and small fittings give the room a cleaner centre.

Finish the bath properly.

The 1.88 × 0.78 m fitted bath has a modelled bathing well, rim, waste, overflow and serviceable limestone apron. Bronze controls and a flexible hand shower sit at the front of the east wall. The existing privacy glass and white window board remain. Towels sit close to the bath, outside the basin approach.

Check the room in use.

The retained 832 mm hall opening receives a new right-hinged inward oak leaf, avoiding the pinch at the WC approach. Six separate use states cover arrival, closed door, basin use, WC use, drying and the extended drawer with a 600 mm body. Door, drawer and bathing-well geometry are checked in the native model. Final fittings, waterproofing, services and measured clearances still need detailed selection.
