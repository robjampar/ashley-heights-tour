BEDROOM 3 / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

A settled bed, a clear outlook.

A slim upholstered king bed, fitted oak storage and a useful place to sit on arrival.

Loading your room…

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

Keep the useful bed wall.

The king bed stays against the solid south wall, with a complete slim frame, upholstered headboard and floating stone-topped bedsides. Individual reading lights, switches and charging points sit on an oak backdrop. Warm wool carpet and ivory bedding carry the house palette into the room.

Make storage fit properly.

A 2.10 m sliding wardrobe replaces the previous 1.70 m run. Its 650 mm depth sits inside the retained skirting. A recessed Roman blind keeps the window and radiator clear of loose fabric. The hall opening and original outward-opening balcony door remain.

Leave room for everyday movement.

The luggage perch sits beyond the entrance turn, so someone seated there does not block arrival. A new inward oak hall leaf fits the existing 778 mm opening. The full bed frame leaves approximately 679 mm to the radiator; this and the wardrobe approach are single-file routes. Six use states are checked with a 600 mm body, and both complete door assemblies are tested through their sweeps. Final products and site dimensions still need verification.

Developed concept for review. Dimensions come from the model. Furniture access, viewing and simultaneous-use checks are recorded separately from construction feasibility.
