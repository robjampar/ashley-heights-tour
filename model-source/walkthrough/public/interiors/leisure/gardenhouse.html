Make the narrow summer house useful.

An upholstered oak bench sits at one end, with a shallow drinks cupboard and a separate changing bench at the other. A gathered ivory curtain provides privacy. Hooks, towel borders, hollow cups, cabinet pulls and individual tool fittings are modelled.

Fit the actual rooms.

The compact WC has a 640 mm basin, wall-hung pan, mirror, bronze mixer and serviceable storage. The small tool store uses a shallow far-wall rack. Existing door apertures stay in place; the complete new leaves are checked through their swings.
