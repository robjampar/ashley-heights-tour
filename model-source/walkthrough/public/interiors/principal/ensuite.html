A place to slow down.

A full-size 1.8 × 0.8 m oval bath sits beside the north window. Opposite it, the walk-in shower has fixed glazing, a rainfall head, separate handset and reachable controls. The WC sits behind a limestone privacy return.

The 1.8 m floating oak vanity has two basins, illuminated mirrors, deep drawers and a lower towel shelf. Warm bronze fittings and honed limestone continue the bedroom palette.

Get dressed in the daylight.

The walk-through wardrobe has six tall bays: double and long hanging, drawers, shoes and folded clothes, with overhead storage. Low drawers run beneath the existing east window, keeping its daylight and opening clear.

There is no central island to walk around. A full-length mirror faces the dressing space, and a proper door separates clothes from the bathroom.
