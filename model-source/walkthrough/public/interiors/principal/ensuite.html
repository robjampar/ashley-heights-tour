A place to slow down.

The bathroom is now 750 mm longer, at approximately 14.8 m². A new window sits behind the full-size 1.8 × 0.8 m oval bath, with privacy glazing and a raised sill. Opposite it, the walk-in shower has fixed glazing, a rainfall head, separate handset and reachable controls. The WC sits behind a limestone privacy return.

The 1.8 m floating oak vanity has two basins, illuminated mirrors, deep drawers and a lower towel shelf. Warm bronze fittings and honed limestone continue the bedroom palette.

Get dressed in the daylight.

The walk-through wardrobe has six tall bays: double and long hanging, drawers, shoes and folded clothes, with overhead storage. The wardrobe keeps its 12.1 m² footprint, shifted 750 mm towards the bed together with its east window. Low drawers sit beneath that window, keeping the opening clear.

There is no central island to walk around. A full-length mirror faces the dressing space, and a proper door separates clothes from the bathroom.
