A proper place for the bed.

The 1.8 × 2 m mattress sits against the existing solid south wall, with a padded headboard, oak wall panels, two 600 mm bedsides, reading lights and reachable switches. The entrance opens into a clear route, with the bed beyond.

A fixed 85-inch-class screen faces the bed directly. A low bench completes the sleeping area without occupying the main foot route.

Somewhere else to unwind.

The 2.3 m sofa has its own fixed 55-inch-class TV, a limestone coffee table, side table and reading lamp. The central route passes beside the seating group.

A 1.8 × 0.7 m desk is side-lit by the south window. The original vaulted window seat keeps its clear central approach; its sloping sides remain low. Ivory textiles, pale oak and limestone carry through from the kitchen.

This is the new isolated bedroom for both proposals. It moves the previous south-east bathroom into the northern wing. The dressing room and ensuite layout, drainage and ventilation still need resolving. The full-house tour retains its previous suite until those adjoining spaces are fitted.
