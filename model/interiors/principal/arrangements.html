Better rules before another layout.

The previous furniture arrangements are withdrawn. A good bedroom needs more than clear walking routes and TV sightlines.

Your requirements are now explicit.

Headboards against proper walls. Fixed TVs integrated into the room. A comfortable sitting area, useful study, proportionate dressing space and generous ensuite.

What changes in the design process

  • Choose a convincing bed wall with room for the complete bed, useful bedsides and lighting.
  • Review the entrance view and the room from the positions where you actually sit or lie down.
  • Keep normal routes clear while chairs, drawers and doors are in use.
  • Test daylight, privacy, headroom and two people using the suite together.
  • Reject a weak composition even when the geometry checks pass.
