One screen. Two places to relax.

The bed moves into the wider north-east bay. The sofa occupies the south window end. Both see the same angled 85-inch-class TV simultaneously, with roughly 3.7 m and 4.3 m viewing distances.

Space for dressing, not a large office.

The walk-in dressing zone grows from about 7.6 m² to 27.3 m², including circulation. A slim drawer island has about 1 m clearance on each side. Tall storage avoids the windows; low drawers sit below the sills.

The work area becomes a 1.5 × 0.6 m desk in a 4.7 m² window nook. The bathroom and separate WC keep their current positions.
