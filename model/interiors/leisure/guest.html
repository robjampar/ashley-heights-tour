GARDEN GUEST BEDROOM / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

A restful room with a garden outlook.

A wall-backed super-king bed, useful fitted storage and a quiet place to read.

A settled place for the bed.

The bed sits against the solid east wall, with an upholstered headboard, floating oak bedsides and individual reading lights. A fixed 43-inch television fits the short wall between the doorways. Full-height ivory curtains, warm wool carpet and a curved reading chair soften the retained room.

Storage without blocking the view.

A 3.675 m fitted wardrobe runs along the south wall, using sliding fronts to keep the aisle free. It replaces the previous 4.24 m run. The window and radiator stay clear, and the reading table sits back from the route around the bed.

The measured trade-offs.

The wardrobe-to-bed aisle is 855 mm. Someone choosing clothes occupies that side while the other bedside, ensuite and balcony remain reachable. The fixed television has a modest image size at this distance. The ensuite door opens into the bedroom to release space in the compact bathroom; the existing doorway stays in place.

Developed concept for review. Dimensions come from the model. Furniture access, viewing and simultaneous-use checks are recorded separately from construction feasibility.
