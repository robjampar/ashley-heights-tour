CLOAKROOM / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

A clearer, calmer small room.

A floating oak vanity, limestone surfaces and carefully fitted bronze details.

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

Keep the shell, simplify the fittings.

A 900 mm floating vanity replaces the previous 1.68 m cabinet run. Its recessed basin, wall mixer and lit mirror leave a clearer arrival. The WC stays on the south service wall, with a concealed cistern and a simple relief artwork above.

Remove what is no longer useful.

The former south window is already bricked up in both proposals. Its old curtains are removed and the inner wall is finished in ivory. The existing hall doorway stays in place. Oak, limestone and bronze connect this small room to the rest of the house.

A compact room for one person.

The retained doorway is approximately 679 mm wide. A 600 mm body has been checked with the door open, closed and the upper vanity drawer extended. The U-shaped drawer clears the basin and waste assembly. Storage is reduced, and basin and WC use share the floor area. Final ventilation, plumbing and purchased fittings still need detailed selection.

Developed concept for review. Dimensions come from the model. Furniture access, viewing and simultaneous-use checks are recorded separately from construction feasibility.
