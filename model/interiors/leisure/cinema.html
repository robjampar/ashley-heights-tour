CINEMA / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

A quieter place to watch.

Deep seats, warm fabric and oak, with a clear view from every place.

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

One comfortable row.

Four generous fixed seats face a 2.6 m screen. A low limestone table leaves a 650 mm approach to the seats; the wider eastern aisle leads back to the door. Cup holders and charging are built into the centre console, with shallow storage for blankets behind the sofa.

An explicit trade-off.

This arrangement has four places, compared with six in the previous two-row layout. It removes the rear platform and keeps other viewers out of the screen sightline. The seat approach is single-file and passing in front of occupied knees still requires people to tuck in. The fabric, oak divisions and low lighting soften the room; sound isolation, ventilation and final equipment remain to be designed.

Developed concept for review. Dimensions come from the model. Furniture access, viewing and simultaneous-use checks are recorded separately from construction feasibility.
