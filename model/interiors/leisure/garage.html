DOUBLE GARAGE & PARKING / ROOM DEVELOPMENT 01

Two cars, useful storage, clear everyday routes.

A restrained warm-grey garage with oak and stone fittings, keeping the existing doors and adding a fourth outdoor space on the drive.

Loading your room…

Drag to look around · scroll to zoom · right-drag to pan. The ceiling is removed only in the cutaway.

Park first, fit storage around it.

The two existing 4.4 × 1.8 m concept cars are staggered, leaving room for an east-wall worktop and closed storage beside the southern car. Complete drawers, tool handles, socket apertures, chargers, coiled cables and restrained ceiling fittings are modelled. The garage opening, window and both house connections remain.

Check how the room works.

Walking routes use a 600 mm body and 42-degree front-door envelopes. The house, utility, chargers and storage remain connected with either car’s front doors open, or both drivers’ doors open. With all four front doors open together, a door must close to pass between the separated areas. This retained garage does not offer unrestricted door opening; the actual household vehicles need checking.

Keep four outdoor spaces.

A fourth 4.8 × 2.5 m compact-car space occupies the existing resin drive, preserving the front lawn and pedestrian court. Arrival and departure are checked with the other bays occupied, including the two garage cars, and the pedestrian route to the entrance stays connected. Vehicle dimensions and surveyed site geometry remain the basis for final tracking.

Developed concept for review. Dimensions come from the model. Furniture access, viewing and simultaneous-use checks are recorded separately from construction feasibility.
